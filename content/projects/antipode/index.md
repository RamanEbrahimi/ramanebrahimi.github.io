##### Overview

Antipode is a 1-on-1, text-only debate platform that anonymously pairs users with opposing views. It offers AI
or human opponents, pre-chat icebreakers, shareable challenge links for social media, synchronized debate timers
and a spectator mode for reading past high-quality debates.

---

##### Incentives

The mechanics reward persuasion over "winning": a change-my-view concession metric and post-chat quality
ratings keep the ecosystem constructive without heavy moderation.

Tools: Next.js (App Router), React, CSS.
